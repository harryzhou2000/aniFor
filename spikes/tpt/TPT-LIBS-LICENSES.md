# Official TPT-libs license metadata

Gate A uses the official prebuilt archive pinned in `PINS.json`. Its ZIP
contains these upstream license notices:

- `fftw3f.LICENSE`: GNU GPL version 2 (as supplied by the archive).
- `lua5.1.LICENSE` and `lua5.2.LICENSE`: Lua.org, PUC-Rio permissive license.
- `jsoncpp.LICENSE`: JsonCpp public-domain/MIT terms as supplied by the archive.

The notices and exact binaries are retained in the project-local extracted
subproject only. This record is metadata, not a legal compatibility opinion:
the full corresponding-source, notice, and GPL obligations must be audited
before any redistribution. The TPT-libs archive is not claimed to be GPL
compatible merely because it is an official upstream build.
