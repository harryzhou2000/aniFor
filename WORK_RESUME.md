# Project status

AniforTPT is a browser particle playground backed by The Powder Toy. Its visual target is physically inspired, stylized realism with readable materials and fluid, gas, and light effects.

The scoped visual overhaul described in [NEXT_STEP_RENDER.md](NEXT_STEP_RENDER.md) was recorded as complete on 2026-08-17. [NEXT_STEP_RENDER_PROGRESS.md](NEXT_STEP_RENDER_PROGRESS.md) summarizes the checkpoint. The older work-resume history remains available in Git.

There is no currently declared implementation milestone. Material and scene tuning, plus validation of the optional WebGPU compute probe on adapter-capable hardware, are open opportunities.
